   DHM Hydrology — Data Watch Chrome Extension (Manifest V3)

  A Chrome extension has been created in the workspace at dhm-data-watch-extension for the Department of Hydrology and
  Meteorology (DHM) Nepal portal (https://hydrology.gov.np/).
  ──────
  ## 🌟 Key Features & Capabilities

  ### 1. Injected "Data Watch" Navigation Tab

  • Seamlessly injected into the DHM top navigation bar alongside the existing tabs (Real Time Data, River Watch,
  Rainfall Watch, etc.).
  • Matches the site's Material-UI styling (active #0f72a9 blue state vs white inactive state).
  • Integrated with hash-based routing (#/data_watch).

  ### 2. 10-Minute Reporting Interval Surveillance in Nepal Time (NPT, UTC+5:45)

  • Automated station telemetry across Nepal is scheduled to transmit at 10-minute intervals.
  • The extension calculates the elapsed time from each station's latest observation timestamp normalized to Nepal
  Standard Time (UTC+5:45).
  • Categorization Tiers:
      • 🟢 On-Time (≤ 10 mins): Normal reporting cycle.
      • 🟡 Delayed (> 10 mins to 30 mins): Missed 1 to 3 reporting cycles.
      • 🟠 Moderate Delay (> 30 mins to 1 hour): Multiple missed cycles.
      • 🔴 Critical Outage (> 1 hour to 24 hours): Urgent attention required.
      • ⚪ Offline / Inactive (> 24 hours or No Telemetry): Inactive station.


  ### 3. Default Sorting: Longest Delayed Stations First

  • Default Sort: Stations NOT showing data in 10-minute interval sorted first (longest delay / overdue time at the
  top).
  • Additional sort options:
      • Shortest delay first
      • Last updated time (Newest / Oldest)
      • Station Name (A–Z / Z–A)
      • Basin Name (A–Z)
      • District (A–Z)
  ### 4. Interactive Filters, Search & Live KPI Dashboard
  • Top KPI Cards: Total Monitored Stations, Delayed Stations count & percentage, Critical count, On-time count, and
  Overall Telemetry Health Rate (%).
  • Digital Live Nepal Clock: Displays live ticking time in HH:mm:ss A NPT with current day and date.
  • Status Filter Pills: Instant one-click filtering by status (Delayed >10m, Critical >1h, Offline >24h, On-Time ≤10m,
  All).
  • Multi-Dimension Selectors: Filter by Basin (Koshi, Narayani, Karnali, Bagmati, Mahakali, West Rapti, Babai, Kamala,
  Kankai, etc.), District, or Station Type (Water Level, Rainfall, Combined).
  • Instant Search: Real-time search across station name (English and Nepali), station index, ID, basin, and district.

  ### 5. DHM Operational Utilities

  • 📥 1-Click CSV Export: Download delayed stations report with Nepali timestamps, delay durations, and readings for
  maintenance teams.
  • 🔄 Auto-Refresh Engine: Configurable polling intervals (30s, 1 min, 2 mins, 5 mins, or Manual).
  • ↗️ Direct Station Linking: Jump directly to station hydrographs and historical charts (#/basin/:id).
  • 🧩 Toolbar Action Popup: Quick overview and search available by clicking the extension icon in Chrome.
