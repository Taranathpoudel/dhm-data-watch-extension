  ### 🌟 What Was Added & How It Works

  #### 1. 📊 Dynamic Level Difference Calculation in the Status Column
  The Status column (in both the River Watch table watch_table and the Data Watch table) now dynamically displays the real-time buffer between the current
  water level and the station's hazard thresholds:

  • Case 1 — Below Warning Level (Water Level ≤ Warning Level):
      • Displays BOTH the remaining buffer to Warning Level and the remaining buffer to Danger Level:
          • ⚠️ -X.XXm to Warning (e.g., -0.85m to Warning)
          • 🚨 -Y.YYm to Danger (e.g., -1.85m to Danger)
  • Case 2 — Crossed Warning Level (Water Level > Warning Level):
      • Automatically switches to display the difference to the Danger Level ONLY:
          • If below Danger Level: ⚠️ X.XXm to Danger (e.g., 0.45m to Danger)
          • If crossed Danger Level: 🚨 +X.XXm above Danger (e.g., +0.32m above Danger)


  ──────
  #### 2. ⚠️ Sorting by Difference between Water Level & Warning Level

  Added dedicated sorting algorithms for Water Level vs. Warning Level difference (diff = waterLevel - warningLevel):

  • ⚠️ Closest to Flood / Exceeding Warning First (diff_warning_desc):
      • Ranks stations with the highest flood threat first (stations exceeding warning levels at the very top, followed by stations closest to crossing
      the warning threshold, followed by stations with large safe buffers).
  • 🛡️ Safest / Farthest Below Warning First (diff_warning_asc):
      • Ranks stations with the largest safety buffers first.
  • Interactive Header Click:
      • Clicking on the Status (⚠️ ⇅) or Warning Level table header in the River Watch table toggles this difference sort directly.
  • Toolbar & Dropdown Options:
      • Included in the River Watch toolbar sort dropdown and the Data Watch sort dropdown.
      • Added a one-click toolbar pill: [⚠️ Near / Exceeding Warning] (filters stations within 1 meter of Warning Level or exceeding it).

  ──────
  #### 3. 📥 CSV Export & Popup Integration

  • CSV Export: The downloaded reports now include explicit Warning Level Diff (m) and Danger Level Diff (m) columns.
  • Popup Quick View: Displays the calculated warning/danger gap snippet for each station in search results.
